Callbreak: Nepali Card Game — Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026 · White Tiger Digital Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Summary

Callbreak does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings (language, sound, music, card sorting) and the display name you type for nearby games are saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Playing with friends nearby

When you host or join a nearby game, the app uses your local network (the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot) to find other players and exchange game moves and display names directly between the phones at the table. Nothing is sent to us or to any server on the internet.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app store listing.
