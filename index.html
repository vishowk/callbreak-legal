Callbreak: Nepali Card Game — Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · White Tiger Digital Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Summary

Callbreak has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data. To give you points, badges and a ranking, the app has a simple account on our game server. It holds a player name, a random player ID, your points and some game counts. You can delete it at any time in the app.

Your account

The first time the app connects, our server (play.wtdigitalsolutions.com) creates an account for your phone with a random player ID and a random name such as "LuckyDanfe482". You can change the name in your profile. The account stores:

  • your player name and player ID, which other players see in online games and on the ranking;
  • your points, badge, games, wins and big calls, and your weekly and all-time rank;
  • which days you played, so we can count how many people play each day. This is kept for 120 days.

This is linked to your account, not to your real name, phone number or email. We don't ask for those.

Moving to a new phone

If you ask for a transfer code, the server keeps only a scrambled version of it. Using the code on a new phone moves your account there and signs out the old phone.

Games you play

  • Play Online: your moves (calls and cards) go to the server, which runs the game. Moves are not stored after the game. Your points are added to your account.
  • Play Now and Play Nearby: the game runs on the phones. Nearby games talk directly between phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. When your phone is next online, the app sends a short count of these games (mode, difficulty, rounds, your finishing place). We use it to give you points and to see which modes people enjoy.

Feedback and reports

If you send feedback, we receive your rating, your message, your phone type (Android or iPhone) and the app version, without your account. If you report a player's name, we receive the reported player's ID and the reason, so we can change offensive names.

Reminders and ratings

The daily reminder is scheduled by your phone itself; nothing is sent to us. The rating window is the App Store's or Google Play's own.

Security and keeping data

All connections to our server are encrypted (HTTPS). The account database is backed up daily, and backups are kept for 14 days. Our server sees your internet address while you are connected, but we don't record it.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account removes your account, points and ranking from our server at once. Copies in backups disappear within 14 days. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete the account, but nobody can use it without your phone or your transfer code.

Children

Callbreak is a card game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If what the app sends or keeps changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
